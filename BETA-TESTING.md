# Beta Testing Checklist

Record console output, audit-log lines, data files, entity UUIDs, and restart results for every case.

- [ ] Fresh install creates all config, data, backup, log, and schematic paths without entities or souls.
- [ ] Guided setup records egg, interaction, ritual center, arrival, fountain, and crystals; validation succeeds without a schematic, WorldEdit, or an internal protection region.
- [ ] Egg and recipe displays survive reload/restart without duplication and repairs never alter physical altar blocks.
- [ ] Event preview reports world, fountain, nearby players, integration state, and confirmation expiry.
- [ ] Four protected crystals trigger the real vanilla beam ritual.
- [ ] ScaledEnderDragon scales the resulting dragon and retains all combat/reward handling.
- [ ] `/sed kill` completes only the canonical event after its rewards.
- [ ] Canonical death awakens the altar and creates exactly three souls.
- [ ] Damaged/enchanted/renamed/lore Elytra are accepted; each selection policy chooses the documented single Elytra.
- [ ] Confirmation consumes exactly the configured stacks and one Elytra; cancellation returns the exact serialized Elytra.
- [ ] Full-inventory refunds are queued, never dropped, and `/dragon refunds` retrieves them.
- [ ] Third claim depletes and removes the display egg.
- [ ] PvP death transfers the exact soul before its cinematic.
- [ ] Natural death counts down and selects one eligible online player.
- [ ] No eligible recipient leaves the soul pending; a later eligible join receives it.
- [ ] A Dragonborn killer follows the configured collision policy without losing a soul.
- [ ] Combat logout follows the configured death policy.
- [ ] Restart during summoning enters safe recovery and never creates a second dragon.
- [ ] Restart during ritual releases/refunds or flags consumed offerings for inspection.
- [ ] Restart during transfer preserves the exact soul and one holder.
- [ ] Every begun initial/removal cast increments instability, including later cancellation/failure; casts at or below the threshold never fracture.
- [ ] A Fractured Akuma/Rev/Lamari has 150 HP, tuned attributes, a global live-coordinate boss bar, safe 45-60 second teleports, restart recovery, and direct unbound killing-blow ownership.
- [ ] One non-target Dragonborn caller produces a 25% backfire chance and loses only that caller's soul on a trigger.
- [ ] Two non-target Dragonborn callers produce a 50% backfire chance; a trigger takes both callers and the target, persists all three 12-hour limbo deadlines, and announces the server-wide Dragonborn blackout.
- [ ] A due limbo soul excludes its former holder, waits if nobody is eligible, then chooses a new random eligible vessel.
- [ ] Away from other Dragonborn, Akuma, Rev, and Lamari each see exactly Wings, Roar, both existing soul abilities, and their fifth ability; all passives and landing/field/pulse behaviors match configured values.
- [ ] Each fifth ability requires 100/100 energy, empties the bar, starts the persistent shared ultimate cooldown, and exposes both selected-ability and ultimate cooldowns in the HUD.
- [ ] Akuma's Trail has a stronger land dash; near water it instead gives three seconds to touch water, then grants speed and temporary Frosted Ice water-running with complete reversion.
- [ ] Absolute Zero presents anticipation, delayed impact, an active Frost phase, and a distinct ending without a real explosion or terrain edit. Every surviving target receives a rotating display-entity ice shell that follows their body and breaks cleanly at expiry.
- [ ] Use full, reduced, and minimal effects while observing an Absolute Zero victim. The ice shell is visible only where permitted, contains no real blocks, and all shell displays disappear on target death, expiry, reload, and shutdown.
- [ ] Reload or stop the plugin during Frost Run. Every unchanged plugin-owned Frosted Ice block reverts immediately, while a block deliberately changed after the effect began is left untouched.
- [ ] Every surviving initial Absolute Zero target becomes Brittle. Zero or cancelled damage does not consume it, the next positive attack triggers exactly one configured Shatter, and unused Brittle expires safely.
- [ ] Shatter produces bounded ice splinters and glass audio. Akuma's Trail strengthens its slow against Brittle, and Akuma's Hush extension remains within the configured remaining-time cap.
- [ ] Build Heat by directly damaging marked prey, crossing prey with Rend, and reaching distinct enemies with Wrath. Confirm the HUD cap and tier changes, the configured same-target cooldown, delayed decay, and full reset on death, logout, soul loss, and shutdown.
- [ ] Repeat rapid melee, bow, fire tick, scripted ability, reflection, weak-mob, mob-farm, and same-entity attacks. Only eligible direct player damage and projectiles build ordinary marked-hit Heat or Rampage, and player/mob per-target limits remain enforced.
- [ ] Cast Rend through one or more valid enemies. Only entities actually crossed are marked, mark refresh never exceeds its remaining-time cap, the simultaneous target cap is enforced, and at least one crossing opens exactly one short recast.
- [ ] Miss Rend completely, wait out its recast, aim away from marked prey, aim through a wall, target beyond range, target a spectator, and target another Dragonborn. No invalid case grants or consumes an attack, passes through a wall, or chains another recast, and the failure feedback explains the required visible marked target.
- [ ] With a valid recast, aim at marked prey and verify the bounded pursuit surge. At maximum Heat, confirm the same valid recast consumes one Predator's Claim, produces its distinct arc, title, and sound, deals only configured capped damage, and cannot fire twice.
- [ ] Wrath of Rev follows Rev as a moving field for all configured pulses. First contact marks each distinct enemy, marked contact builds Heat only under the shared rules, Rev alone receives bounded directional flame cues, and no block changes occur.
- [ ] At Wrath's pursuit threshold, the final pulse makes one small wall-safe surge toward the nearest visible marked target or gives the configured short fallback Speed. It never starts a permanent movement task.
- [ ] Inferno's Wrath presents anticipation, delayed entity-only impact, an expanding bounded ring, active Hunt feedback, and distinct exhaustion. It never creates fire blocks, explosions, block damage, or marks targets without line of sight.
- [ ] During Hunt, damage distinct marked targets and confirm Rampage advances only the configured number of times per target. Each valid reward refreshes mobility without passing its absolute duration cap.
- [ ] Reach maximum Rampage, then use a valid marked Rend recast to consume Predator's Claim manually. In a second cast, do not use the claim and confirm it expires without automatic damage when Hunt ends.
- [ ] Inferno's Wrath still requires 100 energy, starts the persistent shared ultimate cooldown, grants Fire Resistance during its bounded pursuit rewards, ends in configured Burnout, and remains compatible with Thermal Convergence, Volcanic Aegis, and Dragon Trinity.
- [ ] Titan's Bulwark shell consists only of nonpersistent display entities and particles. It grants heavy reduction, reflects only direct melee, applies Slowness, and two active Bulwarks cannot recursively reflect reflected damage.
- [ ] Bulwark stores only the configured fraction of prevented damage, never exceeds its cap, communicates charge through the HUD and rising sound pitch, then converts charge into damage and knockback within both configured expiry ranges.
- [ ] Bulwark's expiry remains a compact defensive shockwave and is observably distinct from Lamari's Reckoning knockup.
- [ ] Repeat all fifth abilities with full, reduced, and minimal effects plus sounds, titles, screen effects, animation particles, and HUD toggled independently. Gameplay remains intact while every disabled presentation channel stays disabled.
- [ ] Observe fifth abilities with several nearby players using different settings. Per-viewer particle counts stay bounded and do not cause a multiplayer tick-time spike.
- [ ] At 51 blocks no resonance appears. At 50 blocks or less, both affected Dragonborn receive one rich unlock message and gain the correct hidden sixth Focus slot without losing a core ability.
- [ ] Akuma plus Rev unlock Thermal Convergence, Rev plus Lamari unlock Volcanic Aegis, and Akuma plus Lamari unlock Glacial Bastion. Verify each has distinct combat behavior and does not edit terrain.
- [ ] Thermal Convergence shows linked orbiting fire and ice, pulls visible enemies during alternating bounded pulses, maintains its debuffs, and ends with one Thermal Rupture launch.
- [ ] Volcanic Aegis displays moving magma armor on both Dragonborn, grants its configured defenses, retaliates against attackers only at the configured per-pair interval, emits bounded mobile fire pulses, and ends with one Aegis Eruption.
- [ ] Glacial Bastion displays moving crystal armor, grants exactly the configured ward charges, consumes one charge per positive damage event, applies the configured reduction once, maintains its mobile frost domain, and ends with one Bastion Shatter.
- [ ] Dragon Trinity displays three rotating soul materials and bounded links to the moving group center. Verify Soul Ascension, the configured number of Trinity Pulses, team healing and energy restoration caps, and exactly one Dragonfall finisher.
- [ ] Kill, disconnect, teleport, reload, and stop during every resonance phase. Tasks terminate safely, active state is removed, and no display entity survives the ability or plugin shutdown.
- [ ] With all three Dragonborn mutually within 50 blocks, every pair resonance is replaced by Dragon Trinity for all three players. Moving any one player beyond range restores the appropriate pair state or removes the hidden slot.
- [ ] Casting a pair resonance charges only the caster's configured Dragon Energy but starts the persistent 720-second shared resonance cooldown for both participants. Dragon Trinity does the same for all three.
- [ ] Change partners during resonance recovery and restart the server. The HUD and ability menu still report the shared cooldown and no other pair can bypass it.
- [ ] The energy HUD shows the selected ability cooldown, shared ultimate cooldown, unlocked resonance name, resonance cooldown, energy, and short combat status without hiding any field. For Rev, verify Heat tier, marks, Hunt time, Rampage, Rend recast, and Predator's Claim.
- [ ] Repeat Rev tracking with full, reduced, and minimal effects and with HUD, animation particles, sounds, titles, and screen effects toggled independently. Minimal effects retains essential Hunt data in the HUD while suppressing nonessential trails and arcs.
- [ ] Absolute Zero ends with configurable Frostbite, Inferno's Wrath ends with configurable Burnout, and Titan's Bulwark applies active Slowness followed by configurable Stone Fatigue. Each downside pauses energy regeneration for its configured recovery window.
- [ ] No ordinary broadcast, menu, history entry, Focus lore, status result, boss bar, or public soul placeholder exposes `soul-1`, `soul-2`, or `soul-3`.
- [ ] Duplicate displays/crystals are removed and missing displays recovered.
- [ ] Backup and restore preserve ownership, lineage, event state, cooldowns, and pending transfers.
- [ ] Every destructive command rejects wrong, expired, reused, or differently bound tokens.
- [ ] Switch BETA to PRODUCTION and confirm force/reset/test mutations are blocked.

Live Paper testing is mandatory for vanilla fountain recognition, ScaledEnderDragon timing, display interpolation, inventory edge cases, chunk unloads, plugin-disable recovery, and performance under player load.
